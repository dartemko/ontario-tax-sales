Off-Grid Recreation Rules in Unorganized Townships

1. Bedrock & Skid Foundations

No municipal building permits exist in unorganized townships. You can pin post piers directly into Canadian Shield bedrock or construct on heavy timber skids over a gravel pad.

2. Composting Toilet / Outhouse

Without indoor pressurized plumbing, no septic approval is required. Outhouses must adhere to a minimum 15-metre (50 ft) setback from water bodies and property lines.

3. Water Draw Regulations

Surface water extraction from lakes or rivers for domestic camp use requires no permit as long as consumption remains under 50,000 L/day.
